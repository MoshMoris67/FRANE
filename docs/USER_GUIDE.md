# FRANE CONCRETE FACTORY — Daily use guide

## The golden rules
1. **Nothing is ever deleted.** If you entered something wrong, **reverse** it (open the transaction → *Reverse* → give a reason) and enter it again correctly. Reversals and edits are recorded with your name.
2. **Money only moves when you record a payment.** A credit sale is *owed*, not cash. A customer paying later is a *collection* — it is not new income.
3. **Stock only moves through recorded events** — production completes, goods are dispatched, damage or a return is recorded, stock is adjusted with a reason.
4. If a number looks wrong, ask the Owner to run **Settings → Data integrity check**.

## What counts as what
| You record… | Money in the account | Revenue / expense? |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice (credit sale) | No | Revenue when invoiced |
| Customer pays an invoice | **Yes (in)** | No — it settles the debt |
| Cash sale (paid at once) | Yes | Revenue |
| Expense (paid now or later) | Only what you pay | Expense when recorded/approved |
| Buy cement/sand (Receive materials) | Only if you pay now | **No** — it becomes stock; cost arrives when used in production and the goods are sold |
| Transfer between accounts | Moves between accounts | Never |
| Loan received / repaid, owner puts in / takes out money | Yes | Never |

## By role
**Cashier / accounts clerk** — *Record Transaction* (orange button) for any money in/out; *Sales → New sale* for invoices (add customer on the spot; take part or full payment); *Customers → Receive payment* when a customer pays (choose the invoices or let it pay the oldest first; print the receipt); **Daily Closing** every evening: pick the cash account, count the money (use *Count notes and coins*), explain any difference, submit.

**Storekeeper** — *Inventory → Receive materials* when a supplier delivers; record damaged stock; update delivery status (*Loading → Dispatched → Delivered*). Dispatching takes the goods out of stock — once.

**Production supervisor** — *Production → New batch*; *Start*; add **Material used** and **Labour / cost** as work happens; when finished choose *Complete*, enter produced and rejected counts. The system works out the cost per block.

**Manager** — approve expenses and closings that need approval (they appear on the Dashboard and in the lists); set budgets; reverse/correct entries; read Reports.

**Owner** — everything, plus Settings: users and permissions, company details, import, backup, audit log.

## Common questions
- **I recorded a payment in the wrong account.** Reverse it (reason: “wrong account”) and record it again.
- **Stock shows negative.** Something was dispatched or used before it was received/produced. Find the missing entry, or have a manager adjust stock with a reason.
- **A customer paid more than they owed.** The extra is held as *credit* and shown on their profile; apply it to a later invoice.
- **Profit says “estimated”.** Some goods you sold have no cost on record. Enter their opening cost or record production batches with materials and labour.
- **The daily closing is short.** Enter what you counted and explain why; a manager approves. Don’t adjust the books to hide a difference.
- **Which numbers can I trust for decisions?** Cashbook and account balances are exact. P&L is accurate when costs are complete. The forecast is only a projection — adjust its assumptions to test scenarios.
